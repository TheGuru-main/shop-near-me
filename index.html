About Shop Near Me

Shop Near Me makes goods, services, drivers, and emergency units discoverable by proximity, category, and relationship ranking.

  • Pay on delivery · walk-in · bulky carrier assist
  • In-app messages for protected deals
  • Heartbeat ranking · search · feed · fairly used market
  • Premium e-invoice / receipts · manifests · ratings · reports
